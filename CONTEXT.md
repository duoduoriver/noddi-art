# noddi domain glossary

- **Project** — a private workspace containing one icon brief and its immutable creative history.
- **Concept Sheet** — a generated 2×2 image sheet containing four candidates.
- **Candidate** — one of the A–D, 512×512 crops from a Concept Sheet.
- **Final** — a Medium or High 1024×1024 icon generated from a Candidate.
- **Revision** — the single successful, instruction-led replacement permitted for a Final.
- **Plan Credits** — credits granted for the current subscription period or initial Free allowance; they expire at the period boundary.
- **Purchased Credits** — non-expiring credits from a pack or an administrator grant, after any refund debt is settled.
- **Paid Access** — eligibility for finals and exports established by an active subscription or historic permanent entitlement.
- **Refund Debt** — the unpaid portion of a refunded pack after available Purchased Credits have been removed.
- **Generation Job** — one idempotent, asynchronous request to create a Concept Sheet, Final, or Revision.
- **Export Pack** — a generated, private download containing a selected final icon in a platform-specific format.
- **Admin Case** — an operator-owned record for an ambiguous payment, failed cleanup, or manual remediation.
